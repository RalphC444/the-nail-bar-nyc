Our story

How the bar came to be.

The Nail Bar started with one idea: getting your nails done should feel like a night out, not a chore. So we opened a cozy little studio in Mount Vernon and did away with everything that makes a salon feel rushed.

Zero rush

One client at a time, start to finish. No conveyor belt, no clock-watching — just your appointment, done properly.

Built like a bar

Comfy bar-style seating, good music, and the kind of easy conversation that makes the hour fly by.

Art that's all you

Chrome, cat-eye, 3D, structured sets — bring a blurry Pinterest pic or a vague vibe and we'll make it yours.

Meticulous, always

Gentle prep, premium products, and shaping that lasts. Around here, the details are the whole point.
